# Simnikiwe Mfethe – Work Readiness E-Portfolio

This portfolio showcases my development across five key workplace readiness competencies. For each, I provide **evidence** (screenshots, documents, or media) and a **structured reflection** using the **STAR technique** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

---

## 1. Business Communication

### Evidence
![Professional Email](assets/business-communication-email.png)  
*Screenshot of an email I sent to a local bakery proposing food donation collaboration.*

### Reflection (STAR)
**Situation**: I needed to contact a bakery to explore donating surplus bread to community feeding programs.  
**Task**: Communicate our initiative clearly, respectfully, and persuasively.  
**Action**: I wrote a concise email with a clear subject line, introduced our mission, explained the simple donation process, and offered flexibility.  
**Result**: The bakery responded positively within 24 hours and agreed to a trial—demonstrating effective professional communication.

## 5. Workplace Etiquette

### Evidence
![Professional Virtual Meeting Setup](assets/professional-meeting-photo.jpg)  
*Photo of me during a team sync for the food waste project.*

### Reflection (STAR)
**Situation**: Weekly remote stand-ups with a cross-functional team (developers, designers, NGO partners).  
**Task**: Demonstrate reliability, respect, and professionalism in a virtual setting.  
**Action**: Joined on time, used a tidy background, muted when not speaking, followed up on tasks, and acknowledged teammates’ input.  
**Result**: Received positive feedback on my consistency and collaborative attitude—showing that etiquette builds trust.
